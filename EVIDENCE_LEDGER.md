# Evidence Ledger

Canonical internal ledger for historical evidence supporting the Marcelo Nicchio hub.

This file tracks what a source actually supports, what remains autobiographical, and what must not be exposed publicly. It is editorial infrastructure, not a public confidence score.

## Handling rules

- Preserve original source material outside public-facing pages whenever it contains phone numbers, addresses, signatures, personal email addresses, account identifiers or third-party personal data.
- Do not publish unredacted employment documents, contracts, CTPS pages or business cards containing personal contact details.
- A business card supports professional association and any explicit title printed on the card; it does not by itself establish exact employment dates, ownership, partnership, client responsibility or performance claims.
- Historical website captures support what is visibly presented on the captured page; they do not independently establish ownership or corporate registry facts.
- Public text should distinguish direct documentary evidence from autobiographical chronology and operational detail.
