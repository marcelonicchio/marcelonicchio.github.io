# Visual archive plan — first public wave

The site should not become a wall of scans. Visual evidence is selected for narrative value, historical coverage and rights safety.

## First-wave candidates

### 1. Folha de S.Paulo / Folhateen — 2001
Use: one restrained cover/detail image plus link to the official Folha archive.
Value: strong external contemporary journalism and immediate visual anchoring of the music/culture phase.
Rights rule: use a limited documentary excerpt/thumbnail only after final copyright review; do not reproduce the whole issue.

### 2. Programa do Jô — 2001
Use: linked video card, ideally with a self-hosted still only if rights/use is cleared; otherwise keep a typographic video card linking to YouTube.
Value: nationally recognizable audiovisual record and direct musical performance evidence.

### 3. Meia-Noite e Uns — RedeTV!, 2006
Use: cropped credit-frame evidence card highlighting “Direção Artística: Marcelo Nicchio” and “Produção Executiva: Cristiano Gonçales / Marcelo Nicchio,” provided the frame can be used safely.
Value: direct role evidence and strong transition into audiovisual creation/production.

### 4. Sinal Verde / Prateleira Cultural — 2003–2006
Use: a small editorial mosaic of 3–4 selected pieces, prioritizing a mix of culture and digital themes (for example Ramones, RSS, Google Earth, Skype/VoIP).
Value: visually demonstrates continuity between cultural criticism and digital systems.
Rights rule: selected excerpts only; keep the full scan corpus out of the public repository unless rights are clear.

### 5. Mirantte News
Use: one historical homepage crop + one article/detail crop identifying Marcelo as director.
Value: documents the early digital-product phase and creates visual continuity from BBS/internet culture into Search.

### 6. Clickland Digital
Use: one historical homepage crop or business-card detail with obsolete personal contact data removed/cropped.
Value: visual anchor for the Search/Performance phase.
Privacy rule: do not publish old phone numbers, street addresses or unnecessary personal contact information.

### 7. Research identity — 2026
Use: clean DOI/repository cards generated by the site itself rather than screenshots of repository interfaces.
Value: machine-readable, rights-safe and visually consistent evidence for the current research phase.

## Visual language

- Prefer editorial crops, dates, source labels and short captions over decorative collages.
- Every archival image should answer: what does this document, why is it here, and what period does it anchor?
- Avoid fake vintage effects.
- Reconstructed imagery must be explicitly labeled as reconstruction.
- Never imply that an AI reconstruction is an original historical artifact.

## Archival master versus delivery derivative

The public repository may contain historically important masters when there is a legitimate archival reason to preserve the submitted or restored source. This is separate from normal page delivery.

Operational rule:

- **archival master** — preserves the historically relevant source/treated file when retention is justified;
- **delivery derivative** — optimized WebP/JPEG used by the page for ordinary rendering;
- pages should not serve a heavy archival master as the normal `<img src>` when an appropriate derivative can preserve the editorial function;
- a master linked only for full-size inspection is not counted as normal page-delivery payload;
- new ordinary photographs should preferably enter the repository as JPEG/WebP when there is no archival reason for a lossless PNG;
- PNG remains appropriate for transparency, text-heavy screenshots/graphics, or justified archival preservation cases;
- do not rewrite Git history merely to recover a small amount of space from already committed historical masters.

This distinction prevents two separate concerns from being confused:

1. **repository/archive weight** — what is preserved in Git;
2. **page-delivery weight** — what the browser may load while a reader navigates the page.

The rule above does not override copyright/privacy restrictions. For example, the Sinal Verde rule above is specifically about whether a third-party scan corpus should be public at all; it is not a general statement that every master must remain outside the repository.


## Placement taxonomy

Three editorial placement modes are used for visual material:

1. **Link only** — the page provides a text link or thumbnail to open the image at full size. Best for scans, documents and material whose value is inspection rather than narrative rhythm.
2. **Link + thread** — the image appears inside the narrative and also opens at full size in a new tab. Best for strong historical photographs, key video frames and major reconstructions.
3. **Thread only** — the image appears as part of the narrative composition without a separate full-size action. Best for portraits and supporting visual material.

The same asset may use different placement modes in different contexts. Provenance labels remain independent of placement: original photograph, video frame, scan, reconstruction, etc.
