## Destination indexation policy

Marcelo's current destination policy:

- Home and language hubs: `index,follow`;
- thematic verticals: destination `index,follow`;
- autonomous Chapter Pages / biography-only URLs: destination `index,follow` when they have standalone public value;
- Publications and Archive: indexable;
- Full Biography / Biografia Completa: destination `noindex,follow`.

The Full Biography pages must remain crawlable; do not block them in `robots.txt`. The destination policy does not authorize a one-shot robots change. Each promoted URL needs useful content, PT/EN parity where applicable, canonical/hreflang, ordinary internal inlinks, sitemap membership and relevant audits updated together.

“O Buscador” does not create a Spirituality vertical. Its own URL is intended to become indexable.

## External authority direction

Material external profiles should progressively use the HUB as the canonical personal-site destination where the platform permits it. The target is factual and semantic consistency, not identical promotional copy everywhere. Align preferred name, chronology, current positioning, publication/version metadata and legitimate cross-identifiers. Privacy cleanup on third-party platforms is operator-side.

### B1 — Entity Page
Turn `/` into a substantive human-readable canonical entity page while preserving clear PT/EN navigation and strengthening existing `WebSite` + `ProfilePage` + `Person` semantics.

Normalize HUB links and compatible identity/publication metadata across ORCID, HAL, SSRN, Zenodo, OSF, PubPub, GitHub, LinkedIn and other material nodes. Evaluate Scholar if useful. Treat Wikidata as community-governed infrastructure, not an SEO shortcut.

### B3 — Controlled indexation
Finish the Home and priority content gaps, then migrate substantive verticals and autonomous URLs to `index,follow` in reviewable batches. Move Full Biography PT/EN to `noindex,follow` only after standalone pages have safe human-visible discovery paths.

### B4 — Structured works and selective concepts
Preserve the rule “one work/version, multiple repository instances”; design/test scholarly-work schema; create standalone concept/glossary pages only when they have genuine reader value. BEST/Kenshoo remains useful pilot history but is not the permanent indexation ceiling.

### B5 — Archive as authority
Continue Coitado do Próximo, audiovisual records, press/scans/credits, provenance and useful archive-to-vertical links. AI reconstructions remain clearly labeled as reconstructions/illustrations where material.

### B6 — Real-world measurement
Use Search Console, Bing Webmaster surfaces, branded/semantic queries, dated answer-engine citation tests and field CWV when available. Metrics inform editorial decisions; they do not replace them.
