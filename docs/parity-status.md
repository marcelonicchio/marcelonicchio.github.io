The first parity pass is complete. This does not mean the pages are final or ready for indexing; rights review, link testing, responsive review and final metadata/indexation decisions remain separate launch gates.

Rules:
- parity means agreement on dates, roles, project status, evidence type, titles and version relationships; it does not require literal translation;
- a factual correction in one language triggers a counterpart check;
- PT/EN thematic pairs should normally become indexable together;
- autobiographical claims, preserved private evidence and public external sources must keep the same classification logic across languages;
- future publication outcomes, DOIs and repository destinations are not mirrored into either language until they exist publicly.
