Folha de S.Paulo — “Órfãos do Rock”

Marcelo Nicchio appeared in a Folhateen cover story published after Joey Ramone's death, discussing punk culture, do-it-yourself practice, Brazil's underground market and the persistence of rock.

PeriodApr. 23, 2001
PressPunk / rockCultural criticism

On April 23, 2001, Marcelo Nicchio appeared in the cover story of Folhateen, the youth supplement of Folha de S.Paulo, published after Joey Ramone's death. The printed cover line was “Órfãos do Rock” (“Orphans of Rock”); the inside article preserved in Folha's official digital archive was titled “Onde estão os ídolos?” (“Where are the idols?”).

The article records his comments on Joey Ramone, punk culture, the do-it-yourself principle, the Brazilian underground market and the persistence of rock.
