1998 — Homens na Cozinha

In 1998, Nicchio worked in executive production for Homens na Cozinha, a television program created and hosted by Fábio Ávila and broadcast by Band. His work focused on fundraising.

Some episodes survive publicly on YouTube. Their credits and episode-level metadata will be catalogued as part of the audiovisual archive; until then, the page does not assign on-screen credits that have not yet been checked.

1998–2000 — film fundraising

Through Nicchio & Associados S/C Ltda., a family company involved in tax-incentive projects, Nicchio contributed to fundraising work connected to cultural and film projects. His biography names Carandiru and Domésticas.

Corporate and commercial documents from this phase may still exist in family files, but they will not be published in full if they expose personal or third-party information. The site will use only the minimum information necessary and, where appropriate, redacted documentation.

2006 — Meia-Noite e Uns

Meia-Noite e Uns was a pop-literature television program broadcast by RedeTV! and hosted by Soninha Francine. In surviving end credits, Marcelo Nicchio is credited as Artistic Director and as one of the two Executive Producers, alongside Cristiano Gonçalves. Dreamsnetwork.tv is credited as the production company.

According to Nicchio's autobiographical account, he created the artistic and visual concept and the narrative structure. Paulão de Carvalho took part in the writing and co-presented the program at Nicchio's invitation. Nicchio also recalls that the theme music was written and produced together with Fábio “Badi” Crepaldi.

Also according to that account, the intended title was Meia-Noite e Um (“12:01 a.m.”), linked to a blog of the same name and to the idea of beginning the program one minute after midnight. Because the exact minute could not be guaranteed in the schedule, the title became Meia-Noite e Uns. The business partnership between Nicchio and Cristiano Gonçalves in Dreamsnetwork.tv remains autobiographical for now, pending incorporation of minimal corporate-registry evidence.

2019 — O Arquiteto da Apoteose

O Arquiteto da Apoteose is a documentary about carnival designer Sidnei França. Material was filmed, but the project was not finalized and remains a work in progress. Nicchio wrote and directed the documentary.

Unpublished making-of footage survives. A future public release can document the production process without presenting the project as a completed or released film.

2021 — Autópsia

Autópsia was developed during the pandemic as a streaming/pay-TV project set in a bar and conceived as a space for conversations about culture, collapse and the end of the world. Nicchio worked as creator and artistic director.

The project assembled figures including Paulo César Pereio, Ângela Ro Ro, Claudio Prado, Luiz Thunderbird, Luiz Felipe Pondé, Eduardo Marinho, João Gordo, Mario Bortolotto and Anselmo Vasconcellos, with Julio Piconi directing and Paulão de Carvalho leading content. The official project deck survives in a private archive; full publication depends on a rights review covering third-party images, marks and other materials.

A sanitized public version of the presentation may later document the project without implying broadcast, acquisition or rights transfers that did not occur.
