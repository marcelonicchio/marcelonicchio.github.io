1998 — Homens na Cozinha

In 1998, Nicchio worked in executive production for Homens na Cozinha, a television program created and hosted by Fábio Ávila and broadcast by Band. His work focused on fundraising.

Episodes survive on YouTube and will be incorporated here as the audiovisual catalogue develops.

1998–2000 — film fundraising

Through Nicchio & Associados S/C Ltda., a family company working with tax-incentive projects, Nicchio worked as a Cultural Producer and contributed to fundraising for cultural and film projects including Carandiru and Domésticas.

2006 — Meia-Noite e Uns

Meia-Noite e Uns was a pop-literature television program broadcast by RedeTV! and hosted by Soninha Francine. Marcelo Nicchio served as Artistic Director and Executive Producer alongside Cristiano Gonçalves, through Dreamsnetwork.tv.

Nicchio created the artistic and visual concept and the narrative structure. Paulão de Carvalho took part in the writing and co-presented the program at Nicchio's invitation; the theme music was written and produced with Fábio “Badi” Crepaldi.

The intended title was originally Meia-Noite e Um (“12:01 a.m.”), linked to a blog of the same name and the idea of starting one minute after midnight. Because the exact minute could not be guaranteed in the schedule, the title became Meia-Noite e Uns.

2019 — O Arquiteto da Apoteose

O Arquiteto da Apoteose is a documentary about carnival designer Sidnei França. Material was filmed, but the project was not finalized and remains a work in progress. Nicchio wrote and directed the documentary.

Unpublished making-of footage survives. A future public release can document the production process without presenting the project as a completed or released film.

2021 — Autópsia

Autópsia was conceived and produced during the pandemic as an audiovisual documentary/entertainment project for streaming/pay-TV. Set at Espaço Cultural Cemitério de Automóveis in São Paulo, the format was designed as 10 one-hour episodes, combining debates with poetry, musical presentations and theatrical performances. The project reached filming and has recorded material, but it was not publicly released.

In the original presentation credits, Marcelo Nicchio is credited with the Original Idea and Artistic Direction and is part of Creation alongside Paulão de Carvalho, Julio Piconi and Eduardo Fabro. Julio Piconi is credited with General Direction, Paulão de Carvalho with Content Direction, Sidnei França with Art Direction and Eduardo Fabro with Executive Direction.

The presentation predates filming and records the concept, references, episode structure and proposed cast and guests at that stage of the project — some were realized and others were not. The filmed material remains in the archive and is not published; the original deck preserves the project's identity and personality.
