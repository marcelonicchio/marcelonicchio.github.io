First Demo Tape Recording - Estúdio Anonimato

In April and May 1994, two new original songs written by Marcelo Nicchio were taken into Estúdio Anonimato in São Paulo to record what became Coitado do Próximo’s first demo tape, later duplicated in hundreds of copies for promotional distribution.

PeriodApr.–May 1994

In April and May 1994, two new original songs written by Marcelo Nicchio were taken into Estúdio Anonimato in São Paulo to record what became Coitado do Próximo’s first demo tape, later duplicated in hundreds of copies for promotional distribution.

Each side of the tape carried one song: Side A: Mulher SP; Side B: Pegue o Seu Celular.

Lineup: Mario Joe (vocals), Ricá (guitar and backing vocals), Henrique (bass), Marcelo Minduim (drums) and Adriano (harmonica).
