First Album: Meu Querido Diário

First original album, recorded at Estúdio Asas in São Paulo and produced by Edson X. Marcelo Nicchio contributed to the concept and songwriting and recorded drums.

Period1997–1998

First original album, recorded at Estúdio Asas in São Paulo and produced by Edson X. Marcelo Nicchio contributed to the concept and songwriting and recorded drums.

The album featured guest appearances by Paulão de Carvalho (Velhas Virgens), vocals; Finho (band 365), vocals; and Grupo Emosamba on the introductory vignette. Several videos from the recording sessions have been preserved.

The work was independently released on CD in 1998.
