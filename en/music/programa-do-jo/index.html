PeriodJun. 27, 2001

Appearance on Programa do Jô, recorded and broadcast on the same day, June 27, 2001, by Rede Globo, with transmission through Rede Globo Internacional. Coitado do Próximo performed Estava Comigo, written by Marcelo Nicchio.

During the interview, Jô Soares joked about the band’s name by pretending that “Coitado do Próximo” was the title of the work rather than the band’s name. The joke was scripted and drew laughter in the studio.
