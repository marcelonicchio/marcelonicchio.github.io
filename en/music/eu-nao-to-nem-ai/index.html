Second Album: Eu Não Tô Nem Aí

Second album in the original cycle, produced by Fábio “Badi” Crepaldi and Marcelo Nicchio, who also recorded drums.

Period1999–2000

Second album in the original cycle, produced by Fábio “Badi” Crepaldi and Marcelo Nicchio, who also recorded drums.

The recording revisited emblematic songs from the band through the aesthetics of that period, introduced new compositions by Nicchio and included a ska/punk/reggae version of Ciúmes, by Ultraje a Rigor.

Released on CD in 2000, the work opened new opportunities for media appearances and live performances.
