Internet & Digital Culture

From BBS culture to commercial internet, hosting and digital products: this axis documents a relationship with networks and online systems that predates the later professional focus on Search by many years.

1992–1996 — BBS culture

Nicchio was an intensive user of BBS services including SP Online/STI and Mandic. This period marked his first sustained contact with digital communities, menu-driven navigation, file exchange, messaging and network culture before the commercial web became mainstream in Brazil.

In parallel, he created Minduim BBS, a personal experiment built with RemoteAccess software and two telephone lines. The project was experimental and non-commercial. No original screenshots, photographs or known backups survive.

1996 — transition to the commercial internet

From 1996 onward, Nicchio began using commercial internet access through STI. This phase overlapped with his musical and cultural work in the 1990s, with artistic creation and network technology developing side by side.

2000 — PSINet and corporate hosting

In 2000, Nicchio worked at PSINet, an international internet infrastructure and hosting company, in commercial work related to Corporate Hosting.

Several team photographs from this period survive and will be incorporated here as the archive is digitized.

2000–2001 — Livraria Cultura

Immediately after leaving PSINet, Nicchio worked at Livraria Cultura, at Shopping Villa-Lobos, from October 2000 to January 2001. It was a summer job selling technical books.

2004–2008 — Mirantte News

Mirantte News was created and operated by Marcelo Nicchio, with Luiz Kechichian as investor-partner. The portal brought together roughly twenty service categories, combining editorial content, commercial pages and location tools.

A contemporary article identifies Marcelo Nicchio as site director and describes its commercial hot-sites, categories and MapLink map/routing integration. A homepage capture and the portal's professional card also survive.

Continuity: digital culture before Search

One function of this page is to prevent the digital trajectory from being reduced to “digital marketing.” BBS culture, commercial internet, hosting, editorial writing about technology and portal development all appear before the specialization in SEM. Search came later as a professional layer on top of an already long relationship with digital systems.
