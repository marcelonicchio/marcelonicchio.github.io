From Mirantte News to SEM

The move into Search began with a practical problem: Mirantte News needed traffic and acquisition. Through contact with Search professionals, Nicchio deepened his specialization in SEM between 2008 and 2009 and completed Google Advertising Professional certifications.

This marked an important shift: digital systems were no longer only infrastructure, product and culture, but also an operational and quantitative field involving keywords, auctions, attribution, segmentation, ROI and scale.

2010–2012 — CookieWEB

At CookieWEB, Nicchio progressed from coordination to SEM Manager. According to his professional biography, he led a team of six analysts and simultaneously managed roughly 22 small and mid-sized e-commerce accounts, including Beleza na Web.

Team photographs and possibly cards and other professional material remain in the personal archive. They may provide historical context without turning the hub into a résumé made of receipts.

2012–2013 — Petlove

At Petlove, Nicchio was hired to internalize the digital media operation. According to his professional account, he built the structure, team and processes and expanded operational scale from roughly 150,000 to 3 million keywords across three accounts.

Those figures remain clearly attributed to his professional account until suitable independent public documentation is available. The employment relationship can be supported privately and through career history, but scans of personal records will not be exposed on the site.

2014 — BEST / Kenshoo

In 2014, Nicchio worked as a Performance Manager at BEST, in an operation connected to the Kenshoo platform in Brazil. His work involved platform onboarding and media-campaign optimization for major retailers.

This phase has a particularly useful public record: a four-part video series of a workshop taught by Nicchio for E-Commerce Brasil on Advanced Segmentation in Search and Social. According to his memory, the training took place over two days, from 9 a.m. to noon; that schedule has not yet been recovered in a public source, but the content and his role as instructor are publicly documented on video.

2015 — Ad.Dialetto / Máquina de Vendas

At Ad.Dialetto, Nicchio took responsibility for the Máquina de Vendas/Ricardo Eletro account. According to his professional account, the assignment involved rebuilding a team and working with a monthly media budget of roughly R$1 million, managed through ROI by product category.

The period has little surviving visual documentation. It remains part of the biography without manufacturing retrospective case studies or unsupported performance claims.

2012–present — Clickland Digital

In parallel with other company roles, Nicchio operated Clickland Digital Ltda., an agency focused on Search and media for small and medium-sized businesses. He describes the company as founded in partnership with Carlos Portela.

Preserved historical material includes homepage captures and a professional business card. The agency presented itself as a “Premium Search Agency” and emphasized Search, ROI, metrics, multichannel work and Web Analytics, alongside badges from the Google AdWords Certified Partner and Google Analytics Certified Partner programs.

Those materials directly document the agency's existence, positioning and Marcelo Nicchio's professional association with it. Founder and ownership language remains attributed to the autobiographical account until suitable minimal corporate-registry evidence is incorporated.

2016–2024 — professional continuity

Between 2016 and 2020, Nicchio worked freelance across different accounts and sectors. In 2023–2024 he returned to a full-time remote agency operation at Driven.cx. These periods will be handled compactly in the Full Biography, proportionate to their place in the trajectory and the surviving documentation.
