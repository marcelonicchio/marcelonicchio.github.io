From Mirantte News to SEM

The move into Search began with a practical problem: Mirantte News needed traffic and acquisition. Through contact with Search professionals, Nicchio specialized further in SEM between 2008 and 2009 and completed Google Advertising Professional certifications.

This marked an important shift: digital systems were no longer only infrastructure, product and culture, but also an operational and quantitative field involving keywords, auctions, attribution, segmentation, ROI and scale.

2010–2012 — CookieWEB

At CookieWEB, Nicchio progressed from coordination to SEM Manager, leading a team of six analysts and simultaneously managing roughly 22 small and mid-sized e-commerce accounts. Beleza na Web is among the accounts named in his professional biography.

Team photographs and possibly cards and other professional material remain in the personal archive. The company will be documented with what survives without turning the page into a résumé made of receipts.

2012–2013 — Petlove

At Petlove, Nicchio was hired to internalize the digital media operation. According to his professional account, he built the structure from the ground up, including equipment, team and processes, and expanded operational scale from roughly 150,000 to 3 million keywords across three accounts.

There is little relevant audiovisual documentation from this period. The employment relationship can be supported by private professional documents and public career history, but scans of personal records will not be exposed on the site.

2014 — BEST / Kenshoo

In 2014, Nicchio worked as a Performance Manager at BEST, the Brazilian commercial arm of the Israeli platform Kenshoo. His work involved platform onboarding and optimization of high-spend media campaigns for major retailers.

This phase has a particularly useful public record: a four-part video series of a workshop taught by Nicchio for E-Commerce Brasil on Advanced Segmentation in Search and Social. According to his memory, the training took place over two days, from 9 a.m. to noon; that schedule has not yet been independently recovered, but the content and his role as instructor are publicly documented on video.

2015 — Ad.Dialetto / Máquina de Vendas

At Ad.Dialetto, Nicchio took responsibility for the Máquina de Vendas account, which managed Ricardo Eletro. The prior team had collapsed; his role was to rebuild the operation, form a new team and work with a monthly budget of roughly R$1 million, managed through ROI by product category.

It was an intense period with little visual documentation. It will remain part of the biography without manufacturing retrospective “case studies.”

2012–present — Clickland Digital

In parallel with other company roles, Nicchio founded Clickland Digital Ltda. with Carlos Portela, focused on Search and media operations for small and medium-sized businesses.

Preserved historical material includes homepage captures and a professional business card. The agency presented itself as a “Premium Search Agency” and emphasized Search, ROI, metrics, multichannel work and Web Analytics, alongside Google AdWords Certified Partner and Google Analytics Certified Partner badges.

Those materials directly document the agency's existence, positioning and Marcelo Nicchio's professional association with it. Corporate ownership can be corroborated later through minimal public registry data without publishing full contracts or personal information about partners.

2016–2024 — professional continuity

Between 2016 and 2020, Nicchio worked freelance across different accounts and sectors. In 2023–2024 he returned to a full-time agency operation, working remotely for Driven.cx. These periods matter less as individual authority milestones and will be handled more compactly in the Full Biography.
