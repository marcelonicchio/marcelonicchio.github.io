Search & Performance

From a regional portal that needed traffic to large-scale media operations: Search Engine Marketing became a central professional axis of Marcelo Nicchio's trajectory from the late 2000s through 2024.

From Mirantte News to SEM

The move into Search began with a practical problem: Mirantte News needed traffic and acquisition. Through contact with Search professionals, Nicchio deepened his specialization in SEM between 2008 and 2009 and completed Google Advertising Professional certifications.

This marked an important shift: digital systems were no longer only infrastructure, product and culture, but also an operational and quantitative field involving keywords, auctions, attribution, segmentation, ROI and scale.

2010–2012 — CookieWEB

At CookieWEB, Nicchio progressed from coordination to SEM Manager. He led a team of six analysts and simultaneously managed roughly 22 small and mid-sized e-commerce accounts, including Beleza na Web.

Team photographs and other professional material remain in the personal archive and will be incorporated progressively.

2012–2013 — Petlove

At Petlove, Nicchio was hired to internalize the digital media operation. He built the structure, team and processes and expanded operational scale from roughly 150,000 to 3 million keywords across three accounts.

The role centered on building the internal operation and expanding media scale.

2014 — BEST / Kenshoo

In 2014, Nicchio worked as a Performance Manager at BEST, in an operation connected to the Kenshoo platform in Brazil. His work involved platform onboarding and media-campaign optimization for major retailers.

This phase has a particularly useful public record: a four-part video series of a workshop taught by Nicchio for E-Commerce Brasil on Advanced Segmentation in Search and Social. The workshop ran over two days, from 9 a.m. to noon, and the four-part video series preserves both the material and his role as instructor.

2015 — Ad.Dialetto / Máquina de Vendas

At Ad.Dialetto, Nicchio took responsibility for the Máquina de Vendas / Ricardo Eletro account. The assignment involved rebuilding a team and working with a monthly media budget of roughly R$1 million, managed through ROI by product category.

2012–present — Clickland Digital

Alongside other company roles, Nicchio founded Clickland Digital Ltda. with Carlos Portela, focused on Search and media for small and medium-sized businesses.

The agency presented itself as a “Premium Search Agency” and emphasized Search, ROI, metrics, multichannel work and Web Analytics, alongside Google AdWords Certified Partner and Google Analytics Certified Partner badges.

2016–2024 — professional continuity

Between 2016 and 2020, Nicchio worked freelance across different accounts and sectors. In 2023–2024 he returned to a full-time remote agency operation at Driven.cx. These periods will be handled compactly in the Full Biography, proportionate to their place in the trajectory and the surviving documentation.
